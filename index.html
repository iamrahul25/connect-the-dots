How to play

Three simple rules. Every level has exactly one solution.

Connect the colors

Drag from a dot to the other dot of the same color to draw a path between them.

Don't cross paths

Paths can't overlap. Drawing over another path cuts it, so plan your routes.

Fill every cell

Connect every pair and cover the whole board to solve the level and earn stars.

Features

A calm puzzle game with plenty of depth, built to feel good in your hands.

🧩

200 levels, 10 packs

From cozy 5×5 boards up to 12×12 challenges, each pack with its own color theme and ambient music.

📅

Daily Puzzle

Fresh Easy, Medium and Hard puzzles every day. Keep your streak going and earn a crown for solving all three.

⭐

Stars and hints

Earn up to three stars by finishing close to the perfect move count. Stuck? A hint draws one correct path.

✨

Soft neon look

Glowing paths, glossy dots, glass UI and smooth animations, with a celebration when you solve a level.

🎵

Sound and haptics

A musical note for every cell you draw, chimes when you finish, relaxing background music and gentle vibration.

♿

Accessible

Colorblind mode with an accessible palette and symbols on the dots, plus a reduce-motion option.

New twists as you play

Fresh mechanics unlock along the way to keep every pack interesting.

Walls

Blocked tiles that paths must route around.

Bridges

Two paths cross here, one across and one over.

Warps

Leave one edge of the board and come back in on the other side.

Teleporters

Enter one portal and jump out of its twin.

Tunnels

Paths can only pass along the groove. Tap to turn it.

Rotators

A corner piece that turns your path. Tap to rotate it.

Keys & Doors

Run a path through a key to unlock its matching door.

One solution
