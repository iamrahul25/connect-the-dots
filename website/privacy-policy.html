Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version: Connect the Dots does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There is no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Connect the Dots game (the "App"), developed by Rahul Kumar ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. It applies to the App on Android, iOS and the web, and to this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. There is no account to create and no sign-in of any kind.

The App does not send your gameplay data, device identifiers or usage information to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives data from the App.

2. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the App saves a small amount of game data in its private storage on your device (on the web, in your browser's local storage). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Data Why it's stored
Level progress: levels solved, stars earned, best move counts and when each level was solved To show your stars and unlock the next levels and packs
Hints remaining To keep track of the hints you've earned and used
Daily Puzzle results and streaks To show the calendar, your current streak and your best streak
Unfinished boards and the last level you played So you can continue a level where you left off
Settings: theme, music, sound effects, vibration, colorblind mode, reduce motion and hint reminders To keep the App set up the way you like it

We cannot see, access or recover this data. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, it is permanently removed.

3. Exporting and importing save data

In Settings you can choose Export save data, which copies your progress and settings to your device's clipboard as text. You decide where to paste it (for example, a note or a message to yourself). Import save data lets you paste that text back in, for example on a new phone.

This only happens when you tap these buttons. The exported text contains game progress and settings only, no personal information, and it is never sent to us.

4. Device backups

On Android, if you have turned on Google backup for your device, Android may include the App's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of your device's operating system, controlled by you in your device settings and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to these backups. iOS devices may similarly include App data in iCloud or computer backups.

5. Device permissions

The App uses only what it needs to play sounds and give feedback:

  • Vibration: for gentle haptic feedback while you play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Audio playback: to play music and sound effects. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Internet: this permission is part of the app framework the game is built with. The App does not use it to send or receive any of your data, and the game works fully offline.

The App never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. On Android, some libraries the App is built with declare extra permissions in the app package (for example a microphone permission that comes with the audio library). The App never asks for these, never turns on the microphone and never records anything.

6. Third parties, ads and analytics

  • The App shows no advertising.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone, because we don't collect any.

The App is distributed through app stores and GitHub. Those platforms may collect information when you download the App, under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover them.

7. Children's privacy

Connect the Dots is suitable for players of all ages. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Deleting your data

Since there are no accounts and nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. You can erase all game data on your device at any time by using Reset progress in Settings, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete Account & Data page.

9. Security

Your game data stays in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no risk of it being exposed from our servers.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add an optional sign-in or online leaderboards in the future), we will update this policy before that change is released and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
