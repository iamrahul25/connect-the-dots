Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version: Connect the Dots does not collect, share or sell any personal information. There is no account or sign-in and no ads. Your game progress and settings are saved on your own device. The Android and iOS apps send anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, which levels are played and how often hints are used) to help us improve the game, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share usage data.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Connect the Dots game (the "App"), developed by Rahul Kumar ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. It applies to the App on Android, iOS and the web, and to this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. There is no account to create and no sign-in of any kind.

The Android and iOS apps send anonymous usage statistics so we can see how the game is played and make it better. These statistics cannot be used to identify you. They are described in full in Anonymous usage statistics below, and you can switch them off at any time. The web version of the game sends no usage statistics.

2. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the App saves a small amount of game data in its private storage on your device (on the web, in your browser's local storage). This saved data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Data Why it's stored
Level progress: levels solved, stars earned, best move counts and when each level was solved To show your stars and unlock the next levels and packs
Hints remaining To keep track of the hints you've earned and used
Daily Puzzle results and streaks To show the calendar, your current streak and your best streak
Unfinished boards and the last level you played So you can continue a level where you left off
Settings: theme, music, sound effects, vibration, colorblind mode, reduce motion, hint reminders and whether usage statistics are shared To keep the App set up the way you like it

We cannot see, access or recover this data. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, it is permanently removed.

3. Exporting and importing save data

In Settings you can choose Export save data, which copies your progress and settings to your device's clipboard as text. You decide where to paste it (for example, a note or a message to yourself). Import save data lets you paste that text back in, for example on a new phone.

This only happens when you tap these buttons. The exported text contains game progress and settings only, no personal information, and it is never sent to us.

4. Device backups

On Android, if you have turned on Google backup for your device, Android may include the App's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of your device's operating system, controlled by you in your device settings and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to these backups. iOS devices may similarly include App data in iCloud or computer backups.

5. Device permissions

The App uses only what it needs to play sounds and give feedback:

  • Vibration: for gentle haptic feedback while you play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Audio playback: to play music and sound effects. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Internet: used only to send the anonymous usage statistics described below (if you leave them switched on). The game itself works fully offline; statistics recorded offline are sent the next time you are connected.

The App never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files, and it does not request a microphone permission. Music and sounds stop when you leave the App; it does not play audio in the background.

6. Anonymous usage statistics

The Android and iOS apps use PostHog, a product analytics service, to record anonymous statistics about how the game is used. PostHog processes this data on our behalf as a service provider and does not use it for its own purposes or for advertising.

What is sent Why
Gameplay events: a level was started, solved or restarted, a hint was used, the intro tour was finished or closed, with details such as the level, stars, number of moves and Daily Puzzle tier To learn which levels are too hard or too easy and which features are used
Screens opened (for example Home, Settings, a level pack) and when the app is installed, updated, opened or closed To understand how players move through the game
Technical details: app version, device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, screen size, language and time zone To make sure the game works well on the devices people use
A random identifier created when the app is first installed To count unique players and sessions without knowing who they are

What we do not do:

  • We never collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID or precise location.
  • We have turned off IP-based location, so PostHog does not store your country or city.
  • We do not record your screen, your taps or what you type, and we do not link statistics to any account.
  • Levels you create yourself with the level editor are never identified in the statistics.

The data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored on PostHog's servers in the United States. It is kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Only the developer can see it.

Turning it off: open Settings › Privacy and switch off Share usage data. The App stops sending statistics straight away. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage, also removes the random identifier, so any new statistics cannot be connected to earlier ones.

7. Third parties, ads and analytics

  • The App shows no advertising.
  • The App uses PostHog for the anonymous usage statistics described above, and no other analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

The App is distributed through app stores and GitHub. Those platforms may collect information when you download the App, under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover them.

8. Children's privacy

Connect the Dots is suitable for players of all ages. The App does not collect personal information from anyone, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The anonymous usage statistics described above contain no personal details, and a parent can switch them off in Settings › Privacy. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Deleting your data

There are no accounts, and your game progress is not stored on our side. The anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked to you and are deleted automatically after 12 months; you can stop them at any time as described in Anonymous usage statistics. You can erase all game data on your device at any time by using Reset progress in Settings, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete Account & Data page.

10. Security

Your game data stays in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Anonymous usage statistics are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and contain no personal information.

11. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add an optional sign-in or online leaderboards in the future), we will update this policy before that change is released and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
