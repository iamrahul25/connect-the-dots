Delete Account & Data

Connect the Dots, by Rahul Kumar · Last updated: October 8, 2026

Connect the Dots has no accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in, so there is no account to delete. We don't keep any of your data on a server. Everything (your stars, hints, daily streaks and settings) is saved only on your own device, and you can erase it yourself at any time using the steps below.

What data exists

The only data the game keeps is stored locally on your device:

  • Levels solved, stars, best move counts and when you solved them
  • Hints remaining
  • Daily Puzzle results and streaks
  • Unfinished boards and the last level you played
  • Your settings (theme, sound, music, vibration, colorblind mode and so on)

We never receive any of this, and we hold no personal information about you. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

Choose whichever option suits you. Deleted progress can't be recovered, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Option 1: Reset progress inside the game

Erases your stars, hints, daily streaks and level progress, and keeps your settings.

  1. Open Connect the Dots.
  2. Tap the gear icon on the home screen to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Reset progress.
  4. Tap Reset to confirm.

Option 2: Clear the app's storage (Android)

Erases everything the game has saved, including settings. The game starts fresh next time you open it.

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Connect the Dots.
  3. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly between phone makers and Android versions.

Option 3: Uninstall the game

Uninstalling Connect the Dots removes the app and all of its saved data from your device. On Android, press and hold the app icon and tap Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play Store. On iPhone, press and hold the icon and tap Remove App → Delete App.

Option 4: Playing in a web browser

If you play the web version, your progress is saved in your browser. Clear the site data for the game's website in your browser's settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Site data) to erase it.

Copies you made yourself

  • Exported save data: if you used Export save data, the text was copied to your clipboard and pasted wherever you chose. Delete it from that place (for example, the note or message you pasted it into).
  • Device backups: if Google backup (or iCloud on iPhone) is turned on, your device may have backed up the game's data. You can manage or delete these backups in your device or Google/Apple account settings. We don't have access to them.

Requests to the developer

Because we don't collect or store any data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request is needed. If you still have questions, or want written confirmation, email us and we'll reply as soon as possible.
